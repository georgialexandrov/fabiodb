# App icon prompt (for an image model)

Design a macOS app icon for "Fabio", a calm, fast database client. 1024×1024,
macOS rounded-square (squircle) icon shape with the standard subtle drop shadow,
no text anywhere.

Subject: an alpine marmot in the sentinel pose, standing upright on its hind
legs, front paws held together at its chest, head turned slightly to one side,
alert but relaxed. It's rising out of a round burrow opening at the bottom of
the icon; the burrow rim is a thin, clean ellipse that subtly suggests the top
of a database cylinder, understated, not a literal database symbol.

Style: flat, minimal shapes with soft, gentle shading, like calm,
minimal macOS icons. Few shapes, no outlines, no texture, no gradients stronger
than a soft light from the top left. Warm chestnut-brown fur, a cream muzzle and
belly, dark brown nose, small rounded ears, calm dark eyes without shine
highlights, mouth closed.

Palette: warm off-white background (#FBFAF8) fading very slightly to #F1EDE6,
chestnut browns, cream, and exactly one accent, a warm red #D9534F, used
only on the burrow rim. Nothing else red.

Must stay readable as a marmot at 16×16 and 32×32: strong simple silhouette,
generous padding, the marmot fills about 70% of the icon's height.

Avoid: cartoon grins, big shiny eyes, hats, laptops, lightning bolts, sparkles,
3D clay look, photorealism, busy backgrounds, text or letters.

Give me 4 variations, then the best one also as a single-colour flat silhouette
(for a menu-bar/template icon) on a transparent background.
